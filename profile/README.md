# Bioinformatics & Data Science Unit · IBFG

**Unidad de Bioinformática y Ciencia de Datos (UBCD)**  
[Instituto de Biología Funcional y Genómica](https://ubcd-ibfg.github.io/) · CSIC – USAL

---

The **Bioinformatics and Data Science Unit (UBCD)** is a research support structure at IBFG whose mission is to strengthen the institute's computational and data science capabilities. Our work is organized around three pillars: **advisory & collaboration**, **computational infrastructure**, and **training**.

---

## Advisory & Collaboration

We serve as an internal consulting service for all research groups at IBFG. Since October 2025 we have handled **57 requests** from groups across a wide range of disciplines — with over two-thirds successfully completed and the remainder actively in progress.

Areas covered include:

| Omics & Genomics | Functional Analyses | Other |
|---|---|---|
| Bacterial genomics & metagenomics | RIP-seq · ChIP-seq | Proteomics (coming soon) |
| Transcriptomics | Image analysis | Metabolomics · Lipidomics |
| Eukaryotic genomics | | |

Requests are managed through an internal tracking system that allows transparent prioritization and status monitoring.

---

## Computational Infrastructure

IBFG operates its own HPC infrastructure, managed by the UBCD:

- **High-performance servers** ranging from 88 cores / 384 GB RAM to configurations with 400+ cores and 1.5 TB RAM
- **Storage** exceeding 100 TB
- **Virtualization-based environment** — individual virtual machines are provisioned to each research group, adapted to the needs of each project
- **Computing server** - A dedicated computing server for CPU-intensive jobs

All code and resources developed by the unit are shared publicly through this GitHub organization. We are also members of the CSIC Computational Biology and Bioinformatics Network (**BCB**).

---

## Training

We offer a **continuous training program** open to all institute members — researchers, technicians, and students — regardless of their prior computing experience.

Current topics include:

- Introduction to the Linux operating system
- Advanced command-line usage
- R programming: statistical analysis, data visualization, and applied statistics

We are currently building an **online resource hub** with tutorials and guides covering these areas. Training is also complemented by individualized sessions tailored to the specific needs of each group or researcher.
